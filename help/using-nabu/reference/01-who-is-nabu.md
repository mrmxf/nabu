## Logo Symbolism

**The Frame** (The Clay Tablet): The geometrical rounded square perfectly mimics a modern mobile app icon while serving as a simplistic rendition of a clay tablet. In ancient Mesopotamia, schools were called edubba or "House of Tablets"
, making this the perfect "Home" symbol for an LMS.

**The Main Icon** (The Wedge): In the center rests Nabu's most distinct and historically accurate symbol: the single vertical cuneiform wedge.

**The Colors**: To ensure this SVG renders perfectly across all mobile devices and web browsers, I translated the ancient oklch() palette we generated earlier into their exact hexadecimal equivalents. The tablet is forged in Yellow Ochre, Nabu's central wedge is stamped in Lapis Lazuli Blue, and it is flanked by two smaller stylistic wedges in Cochineal Red Ochre.
