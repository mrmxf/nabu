1. Sign in as **alice@nabu.example**.
2. On the dashboard, the course *Rise Academy — Summer School* and lesson *Secret
   Agents: Build Your Own AI Team* are pre-selected. Add a **session name** (e.g.
   *Morning newsroom*) and **group name** (e.g. *s7g1*), then **Start new
   session**.
3. Move through the lesson with **Next** — the human newsroom task, what an agent
   is, then the agent toolkit.
4. On the **hunter / hattie / pixie** slides, press **Run** — watch the agent
   "think", then reveal its result. *(These are simulated in Phase 2 — canned
   output, no real model yet.)*
5. Type notes on the discussion and reflection slides.
6. **Reload the page** — or sign out and back in, then **Continue** the session.
   It resumes exactly where you left off, with your notes intact.
