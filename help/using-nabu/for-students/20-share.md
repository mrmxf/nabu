At the bottom of every page there's a **Share this page** bar:

- **Copy link** — copies a link straight to *this exact page*. Paste it anywhere.
- **WhatsApp** — opens WhatsApp with the link ready to send to a friend or your
  group.
- **Review us on GitHub** — if you're enjoying Secret Agents, please leave us a
  ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — it genuinely helps.

The link points at the exact slide you're on, and it keeps working even if pages
get reordered later — so it's safe to bookmark or share.
