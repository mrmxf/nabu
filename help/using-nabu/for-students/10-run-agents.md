title: Run an agent, get a result
params:
  readiness: production
  kind: presentation
---
Some slides have an **agent** — a little AI worker you set going. When you press
**Run**, your job joins a queue and you'll watch it move through:

**queued → running → done.**

When it finishes, your **result** appears right on the slide. If lots of people
are running agents at once, you might see "your group's queue is full" — just wait
a moment and try again.

If the panel says the agent server is **offline**, it's not you — tell your
teacher. You can still read the sample result in the meantime.
