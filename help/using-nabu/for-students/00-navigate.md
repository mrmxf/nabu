title: Getting around a lesson
params:

---
A lesson is a series of **slides** — one screen at a time. You move through them
at your own pace.

- **Next** and **Back** (bottom of the page) step through the slides.
- The **progress bar** near the top shows how far along you are.
- The little tag by "Slide 3 of 8" is the **audience badge** — it tells you who a
  page is mainly for (student, teacher, or admin). Student pages are for you.
- Your place is remembered, so you can come back and pick up where you left off.

That's it — press **Next** to keep going.
